FORMULATION & TRACEABILITY

Show the mathematics.

Transparent five-year, action-scheduling, bi-objective model. The browser uses bounded multi-criteria search on the updated Excel model. The separate precomputed MILP baseline solves each sampled ε-bound exactly, but does not enumerate every integer Pareto point. No preset portfolio is imported.

Definitions & annual accounting

Indices

a ∈ A = actions; t ∈ {0,1,2,3,4}

Binary decision

x(a,t) ∈ {0,1}; 1 if action a begins in year t

Active action

z(a,t) = ∑τ ≤ t x(a,τ)

Annual emissions

CO₂(t) = 4,074 − ∑a r(a) · z(a,t)

r(a) = positive CO₂ reduction, in t/year. Negative means an increase.

Annual EBIT delta

ΔEBIT(t) = − ∑a c(a) · z(a,t) − ∑a setup(a) · x(a,t)

c(a) combines OpEx delta and depreciation from the workbook. The displayed annual cost c(a) combines workbook operating cost change and annual depreciation; investment is not charged to EBIT again.

Indicative annual cash delta

ΔCash(t) = −∑a[c(a) − dep(a)]·z(a,t) − ∑a[CapEx(a) + setup(a)]·x(a,t)

This uses declared depreciation only. Other capital expenditures are assumed unknown/zero.

Objectives & constraints

Bi-objective problem

min ∑t=04 CO₂(t)    /    max ∑t=04 EBIT(t)

Total token limit

∑a,t tokens(a) x(a,t) ≤ 10

Yearly token limit

∑a tokens(a) x(a,t) ≤ 3   ∀t

Implement once

∑t x(a,t) ≤ 1   ∀a

Exclusive groups

∑a∈G,t x(a,t) ≤ 1

Includes one DC relocation, exactly one energy installation across both plants, one North-West transport mode and one API Beta stock level.

Relocation year

x(DC,t) = 0 for t ≠ 0

Sampled ε-constraint solves

max CO₂ avoided, subject to ΔEBIT₍₅ years₎ ≥ ε

We solve 25 epsilon thresholds with SciPy/HiGHS MILP and retain non-dominated candidate solutions. This is a rigorous reference for the unedited 25-action baseline, but is not a full enumeration.

Normalized weighting (illustrative)

Score = w·norm(CO₂ saved) + (1−w)·norm(ΔEBIT)

Scores are normalized over the generated frontier. A 50/50 grade does not mean adding raw euros and tonnes.

Interpretation & limitations

This is an analytical planning dashboard, not the official university Digital Twin. The case permits at most one energy installation; DC relocation requires €90,000 one-off in Year 0. Updated-workbook DC-specific transport figures are selected consistently with the chosen DC. It does not predict OTIF or independently validate supplier, sourcing freight, inventory or distribution interactions. In particular, relocation and transportation impacts were estimated under different network configurations. Browser search is approximate; the separate MILP baseline provides exact solves for selected ε thresholds, not the complete Pareto set. The normalized score does not reproduce an undisclosed grading function. Use the selected scenarios as test candidates for the group's simulator.

LITERATURE · VALIDATION · REPORT

Research-led decision modeling.

Four research references guide our frontier, sensitivity and reliability approach. They inform methods, not the numerical case inputs.

Academic foundations

Planned academic report

1. Introduction and research question

Financial versus environmental sustainability under scarce implementation tokens.

2. Case, sources and assumptions

Workbook extraction, source reconciliation, exclusion of preset portfolio sheets, and uncertainty classification.

3. Multi-objective formulation

Binary yearly actions; CO₂ and EBIT; token, exclusivity, DC compatibility and one-time expenses.

4. Results and sensitivity

Frontier, selected action schedules, energy calculation assumptions, capital ceilings, OTIF risks.

5. Discussion and limitations

Calibration versus measurements, stock holding-cost assumptions, reliability and nonlinear interactions.

6. Conclusion and references

Document validated policy trade-offs and recommended next data requirements.

Workbook provenance and research integrity

Input data is extracted only from updated workbook calculation sheets. Dashboard, Schedule, historical Sensitivity snapshots and Audit sheets are excluded from candidate generation. The final report should not claim global optimality from the browser heuristic or measured OTIF improvements. Model scenarios are analysis, not confirmed Digital Twin outcomes.
