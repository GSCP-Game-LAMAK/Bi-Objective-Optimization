FORMULATION & TRACEABILITY

Show the mathematics.

Transparent five-year, action-scheduling, bi-objective model. The current implementation uses a bounded multi-criteria beam search, so its frontier is approximate—not certified globally optimal.

Definitions & annual accounting

Indices

a ∈ A = actions; t ∈ {0,1,2,3,4}

Binary decision

x(a,t) ∈ {0,1}; 1 if action a begins in year t

Active action

z(a,t) = ∑τ ≤ t x(a,τ)

Annual emissions

CO₂(t) = 4,074 − ∑a r(a) · z(a,t)

r(a) = positive CO₂ reduction, in t/year. Negative means an increase.

Annual EBIT delta

ΔEBIT(t) = − ∑a c(a) · z(a,t) − ∑a setup(a) · x(a,t)

c(a) is the annual cost effect as entered. Energy estimates already include depreciation; capital investment is not subtracted again from EBIT.

Indicative annual cash delta

ΔCash(t) = −∑a[c(a) − dep(a)]·z(a,t) − ∑a[CapEx(a) + setup(a)]·x(a,t)

This uses declared depreciation only. Other capital expenditures are assumed unknown/zero.

Objectives & constraints

Bi-objective problem

min ∑t=04 CO₂(t)    /    max ∑t=04 EBIT(t)

Total token limit

∑a,t tokens(a) x(a,t) ≤ 10

Yearly token limit

∑a tokens(a) x(a,t) ≤ 3   ∀t

Implement once

∑t x(a,t) ≤ 1   ∀a

Exclusive groups

∑a∈G,t x(a,t) ≤ 1

Includes DC relocation, Plant B energy systems, North-West transport mode, API Beta stock levels.

Relocation year

x(DC,t) = 0 for t ≠ 0

Normalized weighting (illustrative)

Score = w·norm(CO₂ saved) + (1−w)·norm(ΔEBIT)

Scores are normalized over the generated frontier. A 50/50 grade does not mean adding raw euros and tonnes.

Interpretation & limitations

This is an analytical planning dashboard, not the official university Digital Twin. It does not predict OTIF or independently validate supplier, sourcing freight, inventory or distribution interactions. In particular, relocation and transportation impacts were estimated under different network configurations. The search is an approximate bounded search, not an exact MILP proof, and the normalized score does not reproduce an undisclosed professor grading function. Use the selected scenarios as test candidates for the group's simulator.
